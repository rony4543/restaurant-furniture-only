## Product Overview

The Ugiti Leather Tub Lounge Chair is a masterclass in soft modernism and understated glamour. Designed with graceful, sweeping curves, this chair eschews sharp angles for a perfectly rounded silhouette. The deep, circular seat cushion is embraced by a continuous, semi-circular backrest, providing exceptional wraparound comfort. The upholstery is a luxurious, smooth leather (or high-grade vegan leather) in a sophisticated, warm taupe/mushroom tone, detailed with immaculate vertical tailoring seams. Elevating the design is the striking tubular metal base; finished in a dark bronze or matte black, it features a unique double-ring design that cradles the seat cushion, supported by four slim, straight legs. Compact yet visually impactful, the Ugiti is the ideal seating choice for boutique hotel rooms, elegant café lounges, and premium waiting areas.
