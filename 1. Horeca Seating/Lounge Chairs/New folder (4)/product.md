## Product Overview

The Rulig Lounge Armchair brings the relaxed elegance of a tropical resort to high-end interior spaces. Drawing heavy inspiration from British Colonial and plantation-style furniture, the chair is anchored by a rigid, exposed solid wood frame finished in a deep, rich espresso/black stain. The defining architectural feature is the side paneling beneath the armrests, which is filled with authentic, natural-toned woven cane (rattan webbing) featuring a classic octagonal weave pattern. This porous material adds immense textural interest and visual lightness to the dark frame. Designed for supreme comfort, the chair includes thick, tailored seat and back cushions in a warm sand/beige fabric, accented beautifully by a high-contrast black and white patterned throw pillow. It is an exceptional seating choice for luxury resort lounges, coastal hotel lobbies, and upscale sunrooms.
