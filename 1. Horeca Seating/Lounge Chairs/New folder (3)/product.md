## Product Overview

The Tetra Sculptural Lounge Chair is a monumental statement piece that fully embraces the contemporary "chubby" or puffy furniture design trend. Completely rejecting hard angles and visible legs, the Tetra sits flush on the floor, presenting as a massive, continuous volume of comfort. The silhouette is defined by wildly exaggerated, oversized rounded armrests that merge seamlessly into a low, enveloping backrest, all sitting atop a deeply padded, bulbous base. Upholstered entirely in a rich, warm chocolate brown velvet (or premium microfiber), the chair offers an incredibly soft, tactile experience. Designed to be the focal point of any room, the Tetra is an exceptional choice for fashion-forward luxury hotel lobbies, creative agency reception areas, and bold, contemporary residential spaces.
