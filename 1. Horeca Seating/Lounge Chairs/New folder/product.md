## Product Overview

The Zarax Lounge Chair is a masterclass in modern hospitality design, seamlessly blending premium comfort with commercial utility. Designed with a chunky, enveloping U-shaped back and armrest, the chair is fully upholstered in a highly textured, luxurious beige boucle fabric that invites touch. It rests on four stark, minimalist matte black metal legs, creating a beautiful contrast between the soft upper volume and the rigid base. The defining feature of the Zarax is its integrated side table; the front right leg extends upward to support a small, round black table surface, perfect for holding a cocktail, coffee, or laptop. Complete with a thick seat block and a plush, loose back pillow, this piece is the ultimate seating solution for high-end hotel lobbies, premium cafés, and modern office reception areas.
