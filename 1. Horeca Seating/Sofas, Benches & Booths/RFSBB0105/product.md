## Product Description — Detailed

Designed to command attention in expansive environments, the Ulate Curved Sectional Sofa is as much an architectural installation as it is a seating solution. The sofa features a striking semi-circular footprint, anchored by a deep, dark walnut-stained wooden base. The exterior is a masterclass in woodwork, featuring solid square paneling on the lower half that transitions into an open, Japanese-inspired lattice grid on the upper backrest. This design creates a semi-private enclosure that doesn't obstruct light or line of sight. Inside this wooden shell rest three plush, pie-shaped seating modules upholstered in a durable, light beige textured fabric. Perfect for hotel lobbies, executive lounges, and premium co-working spaces, the Ulate sofa provides a luxurious, intimate "room within a room."
