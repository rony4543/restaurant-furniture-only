## Product Overview

The Yetab Fluted Upholstered Bench is a compact, highly comfortable seating solution that brings a touch of retro elegance to commercial spaces. While previously described as a chesterfield, visual evidence reveals a striking Mid-Century or Streamline Moderne design characterized by deep vertical channeling (fluting) along the interior backrest. Fully upholstered in a heavy, multi-tonal heathered fabric (dusty rose/mauve tones), the bench features an armless, space-efficient profile with a thick, rounded seat cushion. Supported by four short, tapered solid wood legs, it is an ideal piece for hotel lobbies, restaurant waiting areas, and upscale corridors.
