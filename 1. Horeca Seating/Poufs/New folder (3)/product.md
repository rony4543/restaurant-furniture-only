## Product Overview

The Sansa Metal Framed Stool offers robust, industrial-modern seating designed specifically for high-traffic commercial environments. Completely contradicting previous descriptions of a "cylindrical fabric pouf," visual analysis confirms this is a highly structured, rigid stool. The core of the design is an exposed, architectural frame constructed from matte black flat metal bands. Easy to clean, highly durable, and visually striking, the Sansa stool is an excellent utilitarian seating choice for busy cafés, industrial-themed bars, and creative office breakout spaces.
