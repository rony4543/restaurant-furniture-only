## Product Overview

The Nami Contemporary Black Dining Chair represents a perfect fusion of Japanese minimalism and Scandinavian functionalism (Japandi style). Its defining architectural feature is a thick, deeply curved solid wood backrest that provides excellent ergonomic support while maintaining an open, airy visual profile. The chair features a thickly padded, perfectly circular seat upholstered in durable black PU leather, resting on a robust cross-stretcher base. Primarily available in a sophisticated all-black finish, this chair is engineered to withstand repeated daily use in bustling restaurants, stylish cafés, and modern commercial meeting spaces without compromising on design.

## Product Description — Detailed

Designed for spaces where modern aesthetics and commercial durability must coexist, the Nami Contemporary Black Dining Chair is a striking seating solution. Influenced by Japandi design principles, the chair features a solid wooden frame defined by a thick, sweeping curved backrest that gently cradles the user. The seating surface is a perfectly round, generously padded cushion upholstered in practical, wipe-clean black PU leather. Supported by an X-frame cross-stretcher base, the chair is exceptionally stable. Available in a sophisticated all-black finish (or a warm walnut variant), the Nami chair is an ideal choice for stylish restaurants, modern cafés, and creative commercial meeting areas.
