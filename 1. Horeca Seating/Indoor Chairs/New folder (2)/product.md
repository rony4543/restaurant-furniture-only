## Product Description — Detailed

The Joi Minimal Black Dining Chair is a study in subtraction, paring the dining chair down to its most essential and elegant elements. The frame is constructed from solid wood, finished in a sleek matte black, with slender, tapered legs reinforced by an H-stretcher. The backrest is a simple, beautifully curved rail that provides targeted lumbar support while keeping the chair's visual weight near zero. In stark textural contrast to the smooth frame, the seat is crafted from tightly woven dark cord, offering breathable, resilient comfort. Perfect for modern cafés, minimalist dining rooms, and boutique hospitality settings, the Joi chair proves that striking design can be wonderfully understated.
