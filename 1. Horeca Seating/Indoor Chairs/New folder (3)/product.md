## Product Overview

The Rattan Heritage Cane Dining Chair is a masterpiece of artisanal woodworking, drawing heavy inspiration from the iconic mid-century designs of the Chandigarh architectural project. It features a robust solid wood frame characterized by its striking compass (inverted V) leg structure and thick side rails. The seat and backrest are intricately crafted from handwoven natural cane, which provides breathable comfort and a beautiful textural contrast against the warm, medium-dark timber finish. This armless dining chair delivers an authentic, heritage aesthetic perfect for boutique cafés, premium rustic-modern restaurants, and design-forward residential spaces.

## Product Description — Detailed

Drawing inspiration from mid-century modernist classics, the Rattan Heritage Cane Dining Chair is a study in structural beauty and artisanal craftsmanship. The chair is defined by its striking compass-style (inverted V) legs, which provide a rigid, highly durable base and a bold architectural silhouette. Crafted from solid wood with a warm medium-dark finish, the heavy side rails extend seamlessly to frame the slightly angled backrest. Both the rectangular seat and the backrest feature authentic, handwoven natural cane in a classic hexagonal pattern, offering breathable comfort and a beautiful textural contrast. This combination of strong geometric lines and organic materials makes the Heritage chair an exceptional choice for upscale dining environments, coastal resorts, and design-conscious homes.
