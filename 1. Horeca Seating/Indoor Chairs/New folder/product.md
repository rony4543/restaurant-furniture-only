## Product Overview

The Arden Solid Wood Dining Chair is a classic, refined seating option that draws heavy inspiration from iconic Mid-Century Modern and Scandinavian designs. Crafted from solid timber with a warm walnut-tone finish, the chair features a gracefully curved, horizontal backrest rail that provides ergonomic support while maintaining an airy, open-back profile. The wide seat is thickly cushioned and upholstered in practical black PU leather. Engineered for long dining sessions, this chair is a perfect fit for premium restaurants, hotel dining rooms, and design-forward cafés.
