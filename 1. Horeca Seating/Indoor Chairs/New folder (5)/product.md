## Product Overview

The Elara Upholstered Wooden Dining Chair merges robust structural design with soft, inviting comfort. Heavily influenced by mid-century modernist architecture—specifically the iconic compass leg designs of the Chandigarh era—it features a distinctive inverted V-leg structure made from solid wood in a warm medium-brown finish. The chair offers exceptional comfort for extended dining sessions with its generously padded, flat seat and rectangular backrest, both upholstered in a textured beige linen-like fabric. Its strong, geometric profile makes it a standout piece for design-forward restaurants, boutique hotels, and modern residential dining rooms.

## Product Description — Detailed

Drawing inspiration from mid-century modernist classics, the Elara Upholstered Wooden Dining Chair is a study in structural beauty and organic warmth. The chair is defined by its striking compass-style (inverted V) legs, which provide both a rigid, highly durable base and a bold architectural silhouette. Crafted from solid wood with a warm medium-brown finish, the heavy side rails extend seamlessly to support the slightly angled backrest. For extended comfort, both the rectangular seat and the backrest are generously padded and cleanly inset with a textured, beige linen-like fabric. This combination of strong geometric lines and soft, inviting upholstery makes the Elara chair an exceptional choice for upscale dining environments and design-conscious homes.
