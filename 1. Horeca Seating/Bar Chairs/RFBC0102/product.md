## Product Overview

The Varrens Contemporary Upholstered Bar Chair is designed for refined hospitality environments that require both elevated style and extended comfort. It features a supportive, curved backrest that seamlessly transitions into subtle armrests, fully upholstered in a textured beige fabric. The seat rests on a slim but sturdy matte black metal frame, accented by a distinctive metal band around the backrest, brass rivets, and polished brass foot caps. A continuous tubular footrest ensures stability and comfort for guests, making it an excellent choice for premium bars, modern cafés, and luxury hotel lounges.
