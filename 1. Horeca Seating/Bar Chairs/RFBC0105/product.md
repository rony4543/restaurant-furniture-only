## Product Overview

The Ovetu Minimal Wooden Bar Stool is a practical, space-efficient seating solution for casual dining and bar areas. Characterized by its clean lines and robust wooden construction, it features a slightly flared four-legged base connected by horizontal stretchers that double as footrests. Its unassuming design makes it highly versatile across various interior styles.
