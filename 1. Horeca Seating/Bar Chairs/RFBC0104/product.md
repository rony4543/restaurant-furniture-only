## Product Overview

The Petna Heritage Wooden Bar Chair is an exceptional example of natural craftsmanship, designed to bring warmth and sophisticated comfort to premium spaces. Drawing inspiration from Mid-Century Modern and Scandinavian design principles, it features a fluid, curved solid wood top rail that doubles as supportive armrests. The interior is defined by a continuous, ergonomically shaped padded backrest and seat, upholstered in a rich brown leather-like material. An exposed bentwood back exterior highlights the chair's natural material palette. Complete with a metal-protected front footrest, this chair is ideal for upscale restaurants, boutique hotel bars, and luxury residential kitchens.

## Product Description — Detailed

The Petna Heritage Wooden Bar Chair exemplifies premium organic design and structural elegance. Heavily influenced by Scandinavian and Mid-Century aesthetics, the chair is constructed from light, natural wood featuring a masterful continuous curved top rail that forms both the upper back support and the armrests. The seat and inner backrest consist of a single, ergonomically scooped pad upholstered in a rich brown, leather-like material, backed by an exposed bent-plywood shell that highlights the wood's natural grain. Built for commercial longevity, it includes a reinforced H-stretcher base with a protective metal sleeve on the front footrest. This chair is a stunning addition to boutique hotels, upscale restaurants, and luxury residential kitchens seeking warmth and refined craftsmanship.
