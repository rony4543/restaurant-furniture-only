## Product Overview

The Walnut Bistro High Table is an essential, high-quality seating solution designed for upscale commercial environments. The table pairs a beautifully crafted, edge-glued walnut wooden top with a heavy-duty single-column pedestal base. The tabletop boasts significant natural grain variation and a softly rounded edge for dining comfort. The base is constructed from weighted metal finished in a sleek matte black, accented by a distinctive polished brass collar where the central column meets the circular floor plate. Engineered for maximum stability and space efficiency, this table is perfect for cocktail lounges, hotel bars, and premium cafés.
