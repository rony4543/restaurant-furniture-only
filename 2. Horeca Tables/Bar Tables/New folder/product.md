## Product Overview

The Kylie Acacia Wood Bar Table is a beautiful, all-timber piece designed for elevated dining and casual socializing. Contrary to previous descriptions suggesting a metal base, visual evidence confirms this table is constructed entirely from premium seasoned acacia wood. It features a smooth, perfectly round tabletop supported by four distinctively shaped wooden legs that curve gently inward before splaying slightly for maximum stability. An integrated wooden X-stretcher provides robust cross-bracing, essential for commercial bar-height furniture. Finished with a protective water-based sealant that highlights the rich, natural grain of the acacia, this table is an excellent choice for rustic-modern cafés, boutique bar areas, and warm hospitality settings.
