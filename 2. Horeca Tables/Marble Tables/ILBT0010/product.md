## Product Overview

The Wireframe Pedestal Round Marble Bistro Table is a stunning piece of contemporary furniture that brings architectural flair to any space. Visual evidence completely overwrites previous descriptions of a square table with a brass edge and an ornate cast base. Instead, this table boasts a perfectly round white marble (or high-quality faux marble) top with a clean, unbanded edge. The true focal point is the highly sculptural pedestal base, constructed entirely from powder-coated metal wire/rods. Shown in both matte black and white finishes, this airy, modern table is suitable for chic interior cafés or stylish outdoor terraces.
