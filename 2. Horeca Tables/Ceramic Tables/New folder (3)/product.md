- Using a ceramic insert surrounded by wood offers the best of both worlds: the unscratchable, non-staining performance of ceramic where plates and cups sit, with the organic warmth and soft touch of wood where patrons rest their arms.

- The heavy brass flat-plate base lowers the table's center of gravity, making it incredibly stable in high-traffic areas without being a tripping hazard.
