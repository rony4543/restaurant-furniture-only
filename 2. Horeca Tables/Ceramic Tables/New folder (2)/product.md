## Product Overview

The MC-137 Ceramic & Wood Bistro Table represents a masterclass in mixed-material design. Visual evidence completely refutes earlier descriptions of a simple rectangular table on black metal legs; instead, this is a highly sophisticated, square pedestal table. The tabletop features a central square ceramic tile boasting a premium Calacatta gold marble pattern. This ceramic core is separated by a delicate brass inlay from a thick, beautifully crafted solid wood frame featuring a distinct horizontal ribbed texture. The table is supported by a matching fluted wooden central column, which seats elegantly into a brushed brass collar and flat circular brass floor plate. Blending organic warmth with metallic luxury, this table is ideal for high-end patisseries, boutique hotel lounges, and premium modern cafés.
