## Product Overview

The Weighted Base Round Oak Veneer Café Table offers a clean, contemporary seating solution designed specifically for high-traffic commercial dining environments. Correcting previous assumptions of solid oak, visual evidence clearly shows this table utilizes a high-performance construction: an oak wood veneer pressed over a thick plywood core, leaving the layered multiplex edge beautifully exposed. This light oak top is paired with a heavy-duty, flat-plate metal pedestal base finished in a distinctive matte navy blue. Equipped with anti-wobble leveling glides beneath the weighted circular floor plate, this table provides an incredibly stable, stylish, and warp-resistant surface perfect for bustling cafés, casual restaurants, and modern food courts.
