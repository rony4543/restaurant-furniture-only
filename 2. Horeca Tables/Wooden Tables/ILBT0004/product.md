## Product Overview

The X-Base Round Oak Veneer Café Table is a quintessential piece of commercial dining furniture, designed for durability and clean, minimalist aesthetics. While previously described as solid oak, visual evidence reveals a more contemporary and highly stable construction: an oak wood veneer applied over a thick plywood core, leaving the layered plywood edge beautifully exposed. This light oak top is paired with a heavy-duty, matte black metal pedestal base featuring a classic four-toed X-shape design. Outfitted with adjustable leveling feet, this table provides a reliable, wobble-free dining surface perfect for busy cafés, casual diners, and corporate breakrooms.
