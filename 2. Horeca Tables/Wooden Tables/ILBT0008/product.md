## Product Overview

The Two-Stem Rectangular Oak Veneer Café Table is built for high-capacity commercial dining where stability and clean aesthetics are paramount. Correcting previous assumptions of a solid hardwood slab, visual evidence confirms this table utilizes a warp-resistant construction: a premium oak wood veneer pressed over a thick plywood core, leaving the layered multiplex edge beautifully exposed. This expansive, light oak rectangular top is supported by dual heavy-duty metal pedestal bases. Finished in a matte black powder coat, each base features a flat, circular weighted floor plate. This twin-stem design eliminates the need for four corner legs, providing unobstructed legroom for diners while ensuring the table remains rock-solid in bustling family restaurants, cafeterias, and co-working spaces.
