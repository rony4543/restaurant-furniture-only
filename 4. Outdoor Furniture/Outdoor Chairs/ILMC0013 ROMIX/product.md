## Product Overview

The Romix Hoop-Frame Slatted Outdoor Chair is a masterclass in modern, airy patio design. Moving away from bulky traditional outdoor furniture, this chair is characterized by a distinctive, continuous circular metal "hoop" that forms both the curved backrest and the armrests in one sweeping line. Suspended within this hoop is a bucket-style seat constructed entirely from flat metal slats, allowing for maximum breathability and instant water drainage. Built on four sturdy tubular legs, the entire chair is finished in a durable, all-weather matte powder coat (lacquer), available in stark White, classic Black, and a trendy Terracotta/Rust. Crucially for commercial applications, the Romix chair stacks tightly and securely, making it the perfect seating solution for bustling café terraces, rooftop bars, and modern residential patios.
