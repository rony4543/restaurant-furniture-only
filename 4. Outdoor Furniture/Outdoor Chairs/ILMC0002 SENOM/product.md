## Product Overview

The Porto Braided Rope Outdoor Armchair brings resort-level luxury to any outdoor dining setting. Designed with a modern, U-shaped "tub" or barrel silhouette, the chair features a continuous tubular metal frame that forms both the armrests and the backrest in one sweeping curve. This upper frame is meticulously wrapped with thick, braided synthetic rope in a striking vertical pattern, creating a tactile, semi-transparent enclosure that is both supportive and visually light. Finished in a sophisticated, monochromatic dark charcoal/matte black colorway, the chair offers supreme comfort via a thick, tailored seat cushion and a loose lumbar back pillow. It is the perfect seating solution for premium hotel terraces, luxury poolside dining, and upscale residential patios.
