## Product Overview

The Abra Slatted Steel Outdoor Stacking Chair is the ultimate solution for high-traffic commercial outdoor environments. Built for extreme durability, this chair is constructed entirely from heavy-duty steel finished in a weather-resistant matte black powder coat. The design features a sturdy bent tubular frame that supports a seating surface made of thick, flat steel slats. These slats run vertically down the backrest and curve seamlessly to form the seat, providing an ergonomic profile while ensuring instant water drainage after rain. Designed to be easily stacked for off-season or end-of-day storage, the Abra chair is the rugged, stylish workhorse needed for busy café patios, public dining decks, and bustling restaurant terraces.

- The tubular frame is bent to accommodate stacking, allowing multiple chairs to be nested vertically.
- Floor glides/caps are integrated into the bottom of the legs to protect patio surfaces.
