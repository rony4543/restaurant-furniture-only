## Product Overview

The Cabana Stripe Metal Outdoor Sofa brings high-fashion resort styling to modern outdoor spaces. Entirely contradicting previous descriptions of a "steel grid structure," visual analysis confirms a much more refined, minimalist design. The chassis is a slender, matte black tubular metal frame, accented with classic ball finials on the top corners. The true statement of this piece is the upholstery: the interior of the frame is fully lined with thick, rigid cushion panels forming the back and arms, paired with two massive seat cushions. All primary cushions are upholstered in a striking, wide-stripe pattern (light grey and off-white), creating a bold, "cabana-chic" aesthetic. Complete with matching striped bolster pillows and solid taupe scatter cushions, this spacious three-seater is the ultimate luxury lounging piece for boutique hotel rooftops, upscale coastal patios, and stylish pool decks.
