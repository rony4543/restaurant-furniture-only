## Product Overview

The Mira Criss-Cross Rope Outdoor Sofa brings a sophisticated coastal aesthetic to high-end patios and resort decks. Correcting previous descriptions that inaccurately identified the material as generic "synthetic wicker," visual analysis confirms a much more premium finish: the back and armrests are crafted by stringing thick, nautical-style outdoor rope in a taut, interlocking criss-cross (diamond) pattern between a top and bottom metal rail. This creates an incredibly open, breathable design that won't block sightlines. The frame itself is constructed from sturdy tubular metal finished in a warm taupe/mocha powder coat, resting on elegant tapered legs. Designed to seat three comfortably, it includes a long, single-piece seat cushion and three plush back cushions in a complementary soft grey fabric. It is the perfect anchor piece for luxury poolside lounges and beachfront terraces.
